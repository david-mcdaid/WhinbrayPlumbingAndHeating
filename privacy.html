This notice explains what personal information Whinbray Plumbing & Heating collects when you contact us or use this website, why we need it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Contact us about your data by phone on 087 353 2769 or by email at info@example.com.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, phone, text or email us, we collect what you give us, usually:

  • your name
  • your phone number and/or email address
  • your town or the address where the work is needed
  • details about the job

If we carry out work for you, we also keep the job details, quotes and invoices.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote. These are steps you have asked us to take before any agreement.
  • To carry out and invoice the work. This is needed to perform our agreement with you.
  • To keep business and tax records. We are legally required to do this.

We do not sell your information, and we will not send you marketing.

Who we share it with

Only with people who help us run the business and are required to keep it secure, for example the company that hosts this website and delivers form messages to us, and our accountant. We share it with others only if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer records, quotes and invoices: 6 years, as required by Revenue.

This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking/analytics tools. Its fonts are currently loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information, where we don't need to keep it by law
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • give you your information in a format you can take elsewhere

Contact us using the details above. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
